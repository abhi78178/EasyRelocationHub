Frequently Asked Questions

How much does office relocation cost?

Cost depends on office size, number of workstations, IT equipment, distance, packing needs and manpower required.

Can office shifting be done on weekends?

Yes, many movers can arrange weekend or after-hours office shifting to reduce downtime.

Do movers handle computers and servers?

Professional movers can handle IT equipment, but sensitive data and backups should be managed by your internal IT team.
